Who is responsible

Intrecci is a personal diary for adults, developed by Andrea Ferendeles. Privacy and support: aferende@gmail.com. Android package: com.aferende.intrecci. No account, social network or developer-operated cloud is provided.

Your diary

Names or pseudonyms, characters, dates, ratings, contexts, scenes, notes, emotions and care details you choose to enter remain in an encrypted database on your device. They can contain sensitive information: enter only what you need and respect other people’s privacy. The developer cannot read your diary. No diary content is sent to advertising services. Local statistics are descriptive, not medical advice or proof of consent.

Security and backups

The database key is kept in the operating system’s secure storage. Optional app locking uses device authentication; Intrecci receives its result, not biometric templates. Android automatic backup and transfer of app data are disabled. You can export a password-encrypted backup and choose where to share it. Keep its password separately: the developer cannot recover a lost password or device key. Recipients or cloud providers you select apply their own policies. Encryption does not protect an unlocked or compromised device.

Advertising and choices

Google AdMob banners appear only in the guide. Requests are non-personalized; this does not mean no technical data is processed. Google SDKs may process IP-derived general location, ad interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers for ads, measurement and fraud prevention. No precise-location permission is requested. Google UMP displays applicable choices before ads are requested. Use advertising choices in Settings when available to change or withdraw consent. Refusing does not block the diary; ads may be limited or unavailable. Google’s dialog languages depend on Google and may fall back to English.

Permissions and retention

Notifications are optional, local and generic; permission can be revoked in Android settings. File access is limited to the backup you select. There is no contacts, microphone, camera or GPS feature. Diary data remains until you delete it, delete app data or uninstall. Settings → Delete diary removes entries and rotates the database key; partner deletion also deletes their entries. Delete exported backup copies separately. Ad preferences and Google-held data have separate controls and retention under Google’s policy.

Rights and service providers

The diary works on-device at your instruction. Consent-dependent advertising processing follows the choices shown by UMP; Google may also process data for service security and fraud prevention under its stated legal grounds. Google and its advertising providers may process data outside your country with safeguards described in their policies. GitHub hosts this website and processes connection logs under its privacy statement. This site adds no analytics or advertising cookies. If you email support, your address and message are used to answer your request and retained only as needed for support or legal obligations. Do not send diary contents or backup passwords.

Control and contact

You can inspect and edit entries in the app, export an encrypted backup and delete local data. There is no server account to delete. For access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability or a privacy question about information held by the developer, contact aferende@gmail.com. Applicable rights depend on your jurisdiction; you may complain to your data-protection authority. For Google-held data use Google’s linked controls. Intrecci is intended for people aged 18 or older. Policy changes are dated here; material changes are explained before new processing starts.

Help

Start with the illustrated tutorial, which you can skip and reopen in Settings. Create a person using a pseudonym, then add a memory; all extra fields are optional. To restore, select a .intrecci backup and enter its password: preview and confirm replacement. If local storage cannot open, the startup recovery button can restore a verified backup. For support, describe the issue, app version and Android version without private diary data. No subscription or in-app purchase is offered.
